Overview

This project explores the financial pressures faced by households with children in the UK, examining housing costs, residual income, and social support such as Child Benefit. Data spans 2003–2024 and highlights inequalities across income quintiles and regions.

C1: Additional Weekly Housing Costs

Difference in weekly housing expenditure between households with and without children

C2: Housing Affordability Impact

Housing as a share of total household spending, before and after parenthood

C3: Residual Income After Housing

Annual disposable income after housing costs, by region

C4: Child Benefit Coverage by Region

Families receiving Child Benefit across UK regions (2003–2024)

C4.1: Non-Claiming Families / Other Breakdown

Families eligible for Child Benefit but not claiming, by region

C5: Analytical Chart (Big Data)

Patterns across regions, income, housing costs, and Child Benefit uptake

C6: Family Size Distribution

Histogram of family sizes, comparing uptake vs non-uptake of Child Benefit
