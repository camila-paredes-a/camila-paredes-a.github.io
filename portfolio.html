Week 3: NHS Funding vs. Performance

Rising NHS healthcare spending has failed to improve A&E performance; attendances exceeding 4-hour standards have tripled despite increased investment.

NHS funding increased 213% from 2003-2024 (£101bn to £317bn), representing sustained government investment in healthcare resources.

Yet A&E attendances exceeding 4 hours tripled over the same period, suggesting systemic capacity issues persist despite higher spending.
